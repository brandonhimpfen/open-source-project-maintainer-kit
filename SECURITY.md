## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not disclose a suspected vulnerability in a public issue.

Send a private report using `[PRIVATE SECURITY CONTACT OR REPORTING METHOD]`.

Where practical, include:

- the affected component or version;
- a description of the issue;
- steps or conditions needed to reproduce it;
- the potential impact;
- any suggested mitigation; and
- a safe way to contact you for follow-up.

Do not send credentials, personal data, or unrelated confidential information.

## What to expect

The project aims to acknowledge reports within `[NUMBER]` business days. This is a target rather than a guarantee unless the project expressly states otherwise.

After review, maintainers may request additional information, confirm whether the issue is accepted as a vulnerability, develop a fix, coordinate disclosure, or explain why no project change is planned.

## Disclosure

Please allow maintainers a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address a reported issue before public disclosure. Any disclosure timeline should be coordinated where practical.
